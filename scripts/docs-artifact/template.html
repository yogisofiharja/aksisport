    Just built, not yet committed

    • Recurring Schedule editing and sync (DP-007). A Schedule can be edited from its detail page. After saving, the organizer chooses whether to push the Template to Sessions already generated. Sessions edited by hand are skipped, and dates and times never move. See Recurring Schedule Lifecycle.
    • Cross-Club Session history (DP-009). "Session History" on the Me tab lists every past Session a player joined, across all Clubs.

    Both need a run on a device before they count as verified; the new database queries have not been exercised against a real database yet.

    Known gaps in the shipped app

    • No self-service Club join. Since "Join with Code" was removed, an owner or admin has to add each member by username. Club search in Explore is the planned fix and is not scheduled.
    • No forgot-password flow. Deferred on 2026-08-05. Accounts use a synthetic email, so the standard Firebase reset does not work.
    • No push notification on check-in. Deferred when match generation (DP-005) shipped.
