    Just built

    • Redesign part B: Community tabs. A Community now has four tabs for every role: Home (dark header, join-request callout, Current Focus), Sessions (Upcoming · Past · Drafts, recurring schedules row, New session button), Chat (day dividers, roles after names) and Members (join requests first, actions behind ⋯). Settings, Reports and Leave are in the ⋯ menu; back from any tab goes to the main Home. See Navigation.
    • Redesign part A. Every screen now has the design system's AppBar (root on the tabs, back chevron on pushed screens, ✕ on forms, dark on live scoring; the Session screen's date is the bar's context line). Home shows "Right now" (a live Session as a dark card, then today's, next and draft Sessions as rows) and your Communities, or a first-run page when you have none. Explore, Inbox, Me, Notifications and Settings follow the redesign boards. See Navigation.
    • Recurring Schedule editing and sync (DP-007). A Schedule can be edited from its detail page. After saving, the organizer chooses whether to push the Template to Sessions already generated. Sessions edited by hand are skipped, and dates and times never move. See Recurring Schedule Lifecycle.
    • Cross-Club Session history (DP-009). "Session History" on the Me tab lists every past Session a player joined, across all Clubs.
    • Aksi on web. The web build now shows the same app as iOS and Android, headed for app.aksisport.com. Navigation, dialogs, pickers and the schedule sync were checked in Chrome; uploads, chat and image loading were not. The old scorer stays on Vercel from the scorer branch.
    • Live messaging (DP-021, step 1 of 3). New messages, "is typing", read receipts ("Seen", "Seen by N") and new notifications arrive the moment they happen over a live connection, with polling as a fallback. Inbox shows unread counts per conversation and in total; a Session's Chat tab has its own. Direct and Contact Organizer messages push to people who don't have Aksi open.
    • Blocking and reporting (DP-021, step 2 of 3). Block or report someone from their profile or a direct conversation, or report a message by long-pressing it. Blocking is silent: the blocked person's messages simply never arrive, and Settings lists everyone blocked. Reports reach the owner and admins of the Communities involved, who can remove a reported Session message or dismiss the report.
    • Edit and delete messages (DP-021). WhatsApp's rules: edit your own message for 15 minutes, delete it for everyone for 60 hours, or delete any message just for yourself. Long-press a message for the options.
    • Group chats and Community chat (DP-021, step 3 of 3). Inbox's compose icon starts a chat: one person is a private chat, two or more a group (up to 50) shown by its members' names unless its creator names it. The creator adds or removes people, anyone can leave, and group messages push. Every kind of chat runs on the same screen. Every Community has one chat for all members; owners and admins can remove any message and turn it off. DP-021 is complete.
    • Community discovery and joining (DP-017). Communities are Public (join instantly) or Private (join by request). A Community's location and a User's towns are picked from a list of Indonesia's 38 provinces and 514 cities kept in the database; a User keeps several towns with one active. People find Communities by searching in Explore, in Explore's list for their active town, or through a shareable link. Owners or admins approve requests on the Members screen.
    • Display name (DP-016). Set, change or remove it from the Me tab.
    • Account email and password recovery (DP-015). Sign-up now asks for an email and confirms it with a 6-digit code; older accounts get a reminder. Login accepts username or email, and there is a forgot-password flow. The owner is emailed when their password or email changes. Mail is sent through Resend from aksisport.com. It works locally; staging and production each still need the Resend key, sender address and a Firebase service account configured.

    Schedule sync and Session History were exercised on web against the local database. None of this has been run on iOS or Android yet.

    Known gaps in the shipped app

    • No notifications. Nothing tells an owner that someone asked to join, or a player that their request was approved; both see it the next time they open the Community.
    • Accounts with no confirmed email cannot recover a password. Recovery works for everyone who has confirmed one; older accounts are reminded to add it.
    • No push notification on check-in. Deferred when match generation (DP-005) shipped.
